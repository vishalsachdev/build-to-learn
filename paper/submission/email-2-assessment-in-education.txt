Dear Dr. Joosten-ten Brinke and Dr. Beekman,

I am writing about your Assessment in Education special issue, "Responsible AI in Higher Education Assessment." I realize the submission deadlines have passed; I would be grateful to know whether late consideration is possible, or whether a follow-on is planned, as the call fits my work closely.

The paper argues that when AI makes producing an artifact cheap, assessment can no longer rest on the final artifact alone; thinking must be assessed where it now lives — in how a student frames a build, compares and justifies among AI-generated variants, and reflects in their own voice. It draws on authentic assessment (Wiggins), sustainable assessment and reflection (Boud), and comparative judgment (Christodoulou), and treats viva-style oral defenses honestly as a temporary, scale-limited safeguard rather than a settled answer. It is roughly 6,000 words, conceptual, with worked cases from business education.

If the issue is fully closed, I would welcome any pointer to a suitable home. I am happy to share the full draft.
