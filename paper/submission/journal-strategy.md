# Journal placement strategy — `#BuildToLearn` position paper

*Drafted 15 June 2026. The paper (`paper/position-paper.md`, v3) is a ~6,000-word conceptual/position piece. Primary constraint on placement: most education journals are empirical-only; we need venues with a real non-empirical slot.*

## Decision: journal-first

- **Primary target:** INFORMS *Transactions on Education* (ITE) — regular submission. The only true business/analytics-education bullseye that explicitly accepts conceptual frameworks. No deadline.
- **Prestige parallel:** AMLE "Essays" (pitch the section editor first).
- **Fee-free fallback:** Journal of Management Education (named Essay 6k / Conceptual 8k slots, no fees).
- The gist is unlisted (shared with 4 collaborators) — **not** prior publication. The only prior-pub risk is publishing the 4-article web split publicly; hold that until after a journal decision (or run it only if targeting a venue that allows previously-posted work).

## CFP landscape (as of 15 Jun 2026)

The GenAI-in-education special-issue wave already crested; most on-target calls closed Jan–May 2026. The best topical fit (ITE's GenAI-in-analytics SI) closed Aug 2025. Open calls (Frontiers in Education, MDPI Education Sciences) are conceptual-friendly but weak on the business-education framing. **Conclusion: regular submission beats chasing an open SI.** Highest-value long shot = emailing the editors of closed-but-perfect SIs.
