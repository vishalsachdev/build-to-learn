# Thesis — `#BuildToLearn` / `#LearnToBuild`
*v1 — 2026-05-15*

AI has crashed the cost of building, and the right response in education is not to teach *about* AI but to make **building the medium of learning**. The revised Bloom taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001) puts *Create* at the top as the hardest cognitive work; AI inverts that — novices can now create from day one, which shifts the cognitive load to *evaluating, iterating, and developing taste*. **#BuildToLearn** uses the build act as a trigger: it surfaces the learner's mental model as an explicit testable artifact — first for themselves, then for peer review in a safe classroom, then for the world as a portfolio piece. **#LearnToBuild** is the complementary literacy: the active skill of orchestrating AI to build well, which every learner now needs. Students write their own reflections — writing is thinking, so writing must remain theirs; AI is editor, never ghostwriter. Faculty practise the same loop (**#BuilderProf**), both to build mental models of what they teach and for their own productivity. The repeated loop feeds **Grit-CART**: build empowers → curiosity is triggered → competency and confidence accrue → resourceful experimentation begins → taste develops over reps → grit gets trained. The couplet is for the agentic age what *read & write* was for the print age: the literacy people need when the leverage of intelligence is suddenly distributed.
